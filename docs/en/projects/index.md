## Incubating

### Scintela Matrix

**Model × hardware adaptation matrix with reproducible benchmarks** — answering "which model, quantized how, on which board, under what power budget, performs how". One measured row at a time, permanently credited.

- Scope: Model Deployment & Optimization · Embedded AI & TinyML
- Entry: [Matrix overview](/en/matrix/) · [Hardware Lightboard](/en/matrix/lightboard)
