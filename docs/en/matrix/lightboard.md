## How to Claim

1. Open an issue titled `claim: <hardware model>` in `Scintela/docs`
2. Complete the test following the [contributing guide](https://github.com/Scintela/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
3. Once merged, the row lights up: 🔥 Lit · your name

> Not on the list? Open an issue and add it.
